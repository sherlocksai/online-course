Questions

Before you enroll.

How much time does this actually take?

Plan for 5–6 hours a week. The full curriculum runs about 46 hours, so most students finish in eight to ten weeks at a steady pace — though Basic-tier students often move slower since there's no weekly session to keep pace with.

Do I need any prior coding experience?

Some. Pathforge assumes you can already write basic code — from a class, a bootcamp, or self-teaching — but has never taken a project from a blank file through to something deployed and working.

What's the difference between Pro and Lifetime?

Pro gets you the full mentored experience through your current cohort. Lifetime adds a 1:1 interview prep session and free access to every module Pathforge adds afterward, at no extra cost.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes — if you've completed fewer than two modules, you can request a full refund within 14 days of enrolling. Email hello@pathforge.example and we'll handle it directly.

What do I need installed to follow along?

A code editor, a recent JDK, and MySQL — module one walks through setting all three up from scratch, regardless of your operating system.
